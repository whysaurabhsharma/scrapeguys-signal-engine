# commerce asset: shelf teardown, Gramiyaa, cold-pressed oils on Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart
0 of 5 quick-commerce pages requested returned product content. All 5 were challenge pages.
Instamart 3, Zepto 2, all HTTP 202. Blinkit's 2 pages were not requested: its robots.txt returned 403.

2026-11-20. Documented gap, not collected data.

## Finding
Gramiyaa needs per-pincode price, search rank and stock on the three platforms that carry 35% of its revenue. Today it relies on Nitro Commerce and, before that, on "An intern manually" checking pin codes. A plain, polite client got nothing from any of the three platforms. Instamart and Zepto served challenge pages (HTTP 202) to all five requests, in 47 to 172 ms. Blinkit returned 403 on robots.txt itself, so none of its pages was requested. Zero of 7 URLs returned product content.

## Path
PATH B. No ScrapeGuys API key exists in this repo, so nothing was collected. scripts/probe.py made one unauthenticated request per URL, checked robots.txt first, and stored status and timing only.

## Limitations
- **Not probed:** per-pincode views. All three platforms set the delivery location in the client session (location or pincode selection), not in a public URL, so no pincode-specific page could be requested. In-app search results, where rank is decided, were not probed either: no public search URL was found. Blinkit's product pages were not requested at all, because its robots.txt returned 403.
- **Sources measured:** 7 URLs across 3 hosts, 1 product category, 1 run. Only 2 of the 7 are Gramiyaa SKUs. The full 5-SKU teardown was not possible from public URLs.
- **Naive client, not a hardened one:** no retries, proxies, browser rendering, app APIs or location sessions were tried, by design. This shows where a naive attempt stops. It does not show whether a hardened collector would succeed.

## What this shows about collecting it in house
Every platform refused a plain client on the first request, in well under a second. That was before the hard part: a pincode-scoped session, repeated across every dark-store pincode Gramiyaa sells into, hourly, on three platforms. The intern-checking-pin-codes method in Inc42 is the manual version of exactly that loop. Gramiyaa already pays an incumbent (Nitro Commerce) for it, so the opening is coverage or freshness against that tool. Nitro's actual coverage is UNVERIFIED.
