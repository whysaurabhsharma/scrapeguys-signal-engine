## What the probe found

27 URLs in out/probe_log.json: usable 8, blocked 8, skipped_robots 6, error 4, js_rendered 1.

- **commerce:** 0 of 7 usable. Instamart and Zepto served challenge pages (HTTP 202) on 5 of 5 requests, median 62 ms. Blinkit's robots.txt answered 403, so 2 were skipped.
- **ai:** 1 of 6 usable (Lyst itself). Footshop gave 3 x 406 and 2 challenge pages.
- **travel:** 4 of 7 usable. Expedia gave a 429 on its first request. Orbitz robots.txt redirected, so it was skipped.

What it implies: a plain client is refused on the first request by the retailers and platforms that matter here. Collecting this in house means building a collector that gets past those blocks, then keeping it running. **What it does not show:** no price, stock or fare was extracted from any usable page. No retries, proxies or browsers were tried, so the probe shows where a naive attempt fails, not what a hardened one costs.
